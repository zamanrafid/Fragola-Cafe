🍓 Our Story

A Little Italy in Every Cup, Born from Strawberries

Fragola means strawberry in Italian. Since 2013 we blend classic Italian espresso culture with fresh fruity creativity. Beans roasted in-house every week, strawberry syrup made every morning, desserts baked at 6 AM.

2013Small kiosk opens3 tables, 1 espresso machine ❤️
2018Fragola Special bornStrawberry Cloud Latte goes viral
2026Your cozy 2nd homeTerrace + work zone + events
